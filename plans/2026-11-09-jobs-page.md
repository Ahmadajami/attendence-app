# Jobs page: list, create, edit, deactivate job titles

**Status:** in progress (approved 2026-11-09 with every default; Phases 0–4.1 built 2026-11-09; 4.2 waiting for the user's manual check)
**Date:** 2026-11-09
**Goal:** A **Jobs** entry in the sidebar opens `/jobs`: one page where HR sees every job title
(English + Arabic name, how many people hold it, active or not) and can **create**, **edit**,
**deactivate / reactivate** a job and **see who holds it**. It uses the `job_title` table that
already exists, so the page needs **no migration**.

**Lane:** one agent on `app/main` (or a branch `app/jobs` cut from it). One commit per phase,
with the plan file. Start from the first unticked box.

**Before you start, read:** `CLAUDE.md` in full (§1 personal data, §3 UI rules + Forms for every
`.svelte` change, §5 plans, §6 decisions log, §8 hand-off), then
`plans/2026-11-01-employee-profile.md` (H6 job title, H10 never delete) and
`plans/2026-11-01-employee-page-redesign.md` (V5 lists managed where used, V9 picker selects the
new item).

## 0. Starting point (checked 2026-11-09)

- **Table:** `job_title` in `src/lib/server/db/schema/workforce.ts:30` (`id`, `name_en` unique,
  `name_ar`, `active`, timestamps). `staff.job_title_id` → `job_title.id`, `on delete restrict`,
  indexed (`staff_job_title_id_idx`). The newest migration is `0021_staff_legacy_badge_no`.
- **Service:** `src/lib/server/services/staff-lists/staff-lists.{bl,crud}.ts` already creates,
  renames and (de)activates job titles (`kind = 'job_title'`), with audit rows and a
  `duplicate_name` `ConflictError`. Permission `staff.write`.
- **Shared form + actions:** `src/lib/server/staff-list-forms.ts` (`staffListForm()`,
  `saveListItemAction`, `setListItemActiveAction`); schema
  `src/lib/schemas/staff-lists.schema.ts` (`staffListFormSchema`, `setStaffListActiveSchema`).
- **The select the user means:** `/employees/new` → section *Job* → **Job title**
  (`src/lib/app/staff/StaffCreateForm.svelte:207`, `ListSelect` with `kind="job_title"`). It
  offers the **active** job titles, a "No job title" item, and ends with **"+ New job title"**
  (opens `ListItemSheet`; the saved job is selected). It cannot rename or deactivate a job; the
  create page has no "Manage job titles" button. `/jobs` manages exactly the rows this select
  offers.
- **UI today:** job titles are managed only **where they are used** (V5): the "+ New job title"
  item in the job title picker and the "Manage job titles" sheet on the employee's Personal tab
  (`src/lib/app/staff-lists/{ListItemSheet,ListManageSheet,StaffListsHost}.svelte`). `/staff-lists`
  was removed by V5. There is **no page** that lists the jobs with their head count.
- **Sidebar:** `src/lib/app/layout/nav.ts` (`dashboardNav`, `staff.write` on the HR pages).
- **CRUD page to copy:** `src/routes/(dashboard)/departments/` (Card + Table + row
  `DropdownMenu` with Edit / Deactivate, a form sheet, a hidden `setActive` form with
  `toastNotice`).
- Job titles are **not personal data** (profile plan §0); the people list on the page is, so it
  is only shown in the app, never in code, tests, plans or chat.

## 1. Decisions (approved 2026-11-09; stop and ask before departing from one)

| # | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| JB1 | **"Jobs" = the existing job titles** (`job_title`), not a new table: the options of the **Job title select on `/employees/new`** (and on the profile's Personal tab). Confirmed by the user 2026-11-09 (Q-JB1) | One list; the employee pickers and the new page show the same rows |
| JB2 | **Sidebar entry "Jobs" → `/jobs`**, right after Employees, phosphor `BriefcaseIcon` (already the job icon on the profile), permission `staff.write` (Q-JB2) | HR finds it next to the people it describes |
| JB3 | **Fields stay as they are:** English name (unique), Arabic name, active. No code, description, department or grade in this plan (Q-JB3) | No migration; extra fields can come in a later plan once HR names them |
| JB4 | **Delete = deactivate** (CLAUDE.md rule "never hard-delete", profile H10). A deactivated job disappears from the pickers; people who hold it keep it and it shows "inactive" on their profile. Reactivate from the same menu (Q-JB4) | History and reports keep the name; `on delete restrict` would refuse anyway once someone holds it |
| JB5 | **Deactivating a job that active people still hold is allowed**, but the menu item shows the count ("3 people keep this job") and the toast repeats it (Q-JB5) | HR may retire a title before moving everyone off it; the count prevents surprises |
| JB6 | **Table columns:** Job (name in the current language, the other language under it), People (active holders; inactive holders in a muted "+2 inactive"), Status badge, row menu (Edit · People · Deactivate / Activate). Search box (client-side, both languages) and a status filter **Active / Inactive / All** (GET form, `?status=`, default Active, like the employees page, Q-T6) | Same look and behaviour as the departments and employees pages |
| JB7 | **People sheet:** "People" in the row menu (or a click on the count) opens a read-only sheet listing the active holders (name, employee number, team), each linking to `/employees/[id]?tab=personal`; inactive holders in a collapsed second list (Q-JB6) | "Who holds this job?" without leaving the page |
| JB8 | **Reuse, don't copy:** the page loads `staffListForm()` and mounts `saveListItem` / `setListItemActive` from `staff-list-forms.ts`, and opens the existing `ListItemSheet` (kind `job_title`) for create and edit. The employee-side "+ New job title" and "Manage job titles" stay; "Manage job titles" gets a link "Open the Jobs page" | One service, one form, one audit trail; V5 still holds where jobs are picked |
