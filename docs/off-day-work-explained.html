Work outside the roster, explained simply

Sometimes people don't work exactly what the roster says. This page shows the four situations the urgent plan fixes. For each one: what the app does today, what goes wrong, and what I recommend.

URGENT This plan must be finished before the October punches for the Operations team are imported.

How to read the pictures
the roster's plan hours worked partial leave counted wrongly day off (striped)

The dark line is midnight. The examples are made up: a day shift is 08:00–20:00 and a night shift is 20:00–08:00 (12 hours each).

TodayThe app makes you pick a shift for every punch, so you must choose one nobody worked.
What goes wrongThe screens show a shift name that is not true. The pay is right, but only by luck.
RecommendedSave it with no shift. The screens say "Day-off work 13:00–19:45, 6 h 45 min". The pay stays exactly the same.

"No shift" is allowed only on a day with no planned shift (a day off, a swap-off day or a holiday). A normal work day still needs a shift.

TodayThe app compares the 20:00 check-in with the planned 08:00 start.
What goes wrongThe person shows as 720 minutes late, which is false. And the existing swaps can't fix it: they move days off, not shifts.
RecommendedThe app stops and says "These times fit the night shift, not the planned day shift." A Shift change button opens a new request, already filled in. You approve it and the punches save as a night shift: late 0.

A person who is simply very late on their own shift (in at 11:30 on a day shift) is not treated as a shift change. They stay late, as they should. The rule: only when both times fit another shift and not the planned one.

TodayCorrect when typed: the whole night is saved on Monday, so Tuesday stays an empty day off with no overtime. (I checked this in the code.)
What could go wrongThe new "no shift on a day off" rule could read Tuesday 00:00–08:00 as day-off work and pay it as overtime. Punch files often split a night at midnight, which is exactly how this would happen.
RecommendedA night shift always belongs to the day it starts. The morning hours on Tuesday count for Monday. Tuesday shows nothing: no day-off work, no flag, no overtime.

The details

  • Overtime: 12 hours worked on a 12-hour shift is 0 overtime. If they stay until 09:00, that extra hour is Monday's normal overtime (by Monday's pay rules), not day-off pay.
  • Typing: if someone types 00:00–08:00 on Tuesday by mistake, the app refuses: "This belongs to Monday's night shift." A link opens Monday.
  • Import: if the file has a Tuesday row with only the 08:00 check-out, or splits the night at midnight, the app joins it to Monday's night and shows this in the preview.
  • Holiday: if Tuesday is a holiday instead of a day off, the Monday night hours still count as normal hours.

What could go wrongThat plan only allows leave on a work day. Read too strictly, it would refuse this leave because 04:00–08:00 falls on Tuesday, a day off.
RecommendedThe leave is part of Monday's shift, so you add it on Monday. Monday shows 8 h worked plus 4 h leave, and not "left early". Tuesday stays empty.

I have already added this rule and a test for it to the partial-leave plan. If the leave is paid, it comes off the yearly balance as part of Monday: 4 h of a 12 h shift = 0.33 day.

The new "Shift change" request

This is the one new thing you will use. It works like the swaps you already have:

  1. On Swaps (/changes), choose Shift change: one person, one date, the new shift and a reason. Or click the Shift change button that appears when the app refuses a punch, and everything is filled in for you.
  2. The request is pending. Approve it, or reject it with a note.
  3. Once it is approved, the roster for that day says night shift, and the punches save with the right numbers.
  4. Cancelling it puts the roster back. Every step shows in the day's History.

For the October import, the preview lists the rows that need a shift change, with one button to create all those requests. You approve them, run the import again, and the rows save. Nothing is retyped.

My recommendation, in short

  1. Day-off work is saved with no shift and shown as "Day-off work". The pay doesn't change.
  2. A different shift on a work day is never shown as late. Fix the roster first with a Shift change, then the punches save.
  3. A night shift belongs to the day it starts. When the next day is a day off, that day stays empty, with no overtime.
  4. Partial leave on a night is entered on the night's day and counted there, even when its hours fall on a day off.
  5. Build partial leave (its own plan) right after this plan.
