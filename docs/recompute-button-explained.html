ماذا يفعل زر «إعادة الحساب»؟

باختصار: يملأ أو يصحّح السجلات اليومية المحفوظة من التاريخ الذي تختاره حتى اليوم. لا يغيّر البصمات ولا الجداول أبداً. كل الأشخاص والأرقام في هذه الصفحة وهمية.

What does the “Recompute” button do?

In short: it fills in or fixes the stored daily records from the date you pick up to today. It never changes punches or rosters. Every person and number on this page is made up.

3When do you need the button?

When a day's record was not updated automatically. The usual cases:

a) Punches typed before this feature existed: no record yet

Employee “B” (made up) has old punches on 10-28 and 10-29, but no daily record for them:

DatePunchesRecord beforeAfter pressing
10-2807:00 → 19:00none—
10-2807:00 → 19:00—present, worked 720 new
10-2907:05 → (no out)none—
10-2907:05 → (no out)—no worked time (check-out missing) new

Each new record gets one edit-log line: created, source system.

b) An update failed: the day stayed “needs recompute”

If a correction is saved but the update after it fails (a dropped connection, say), the old record stays, flagged “needs recompute”:

DateWorked beforeWorked afterWhat happened
10-30720 stale735fixed, with a log line “worked: 720 → 735”

c) Someone moved to another team or department

A move takes effect from the moment it is saved and does not recompute past days by itself. If the stored days should follow the new situation, press the button from the right date.

4ماذا يحدث بالضبط عند الضغط؟

  1. من؟ كل الموظفين النشطين، وكل من له بصمات في المدة.
  2. أي أيام؟ من التاريخ الذي تختاره حتى اليوم (400 يوم على الأكثر). الأيام القادمة لا تُحفظ.
  3. لكل شخص ولكل يوم يحسب السجل من جديد من: النمط، الغيابات، العطل، التبديلات، البصمات.
  4. يقارن بالسجل المحفوظ:
    • مطابق ← لا يكتب شيئاً، ويُعدّ «دون تغيير».
    • غير موجود ← يكتبه، مع سطر «أُنشئ».
    • مختلف ← يصححه، مع سطر لكل قيمة تغيّرت (القديم ← الجديد).
  5. يكتب سطراً واحداً في سجل التدقيق بالمدة والأعداد، ويُظهر رسالة.

مثال: 5 موظفين وهميين، الضغط من 25-10 واليوم 02-11

من 25-10 إلى 31-10 لا سجلات (7 أيام × 5 = 35)، ويوما 01-11 و02-11 صحيحان (2 × 5 = 10):

✓ أُعيد الحساب: 35 يوماً كُتب، 10 دون تغيير، 35 سطراً في السجل.

ثم الضغط مرة ثانية بالتاريخ نفسه:

✓ أُعيد الحساب: 0 يوماً كُتب، 45 دون تغيير، 0 سطراً في السجل.

الضغطة الثانية لا تكتب شيئاً: هذا هو المتوقع، وهو دليل أن كل السجلات صحيحة.

ما لا يفعله الزر أبداً

لا يغيّر البصمات
لا يغيّر الأنماط أو الجداول
لا يغيّر الغيابات أو العطل
لا يحفظ أياماً قادمة
لا يطبّق نمطاً على تواريخ ماضية

4What exactly happens when you press it?

  1. Who: everyone active, plus anyone with punches in the range.
  2. Which days: from the date you pick up to today (at most 400 days). Future days are never stored.
  3. For each person and day it works the record out again from the pattern, absences, holidays, swaps and punches.
  4. It compares with the stored record:
    • the same → nothing written; counts as “unchanged”
    • missing → written, with a “created” line
    • different → fixed, with one line per changed value (old → new)
  5. It writes one audit row with the range and the counts, and shows a toast.

Example: 5 made-up people, pressed from 10-25, today is 11-02

10-25 to 10-31 have no records (7 days × 5 = 35); 11-01 and 11-02 are already right (2 × 5 = 10):

✓ Recomputed: 35 days written, 10 unchanged, 35 log lines.

Then pressing again with the same date:

✓ Recomputed: 0 days written, 45 unchanged, 0 log lines.

The second press writes nothing. That is expected: it proves every record is right.

What the button never does

never changes punches
never changes patterns or rosters
never changes absences or holidays
never stores future days
never applies a pattern to past dates

You created a pattern (Friday and Saturday off), made the employee follow it, then pressed the button. In order:

1. Pattern saved from 11-02 (today)a pattern starts today, not before
→
2. Automatic update at oncetoday's record changed by itself (source: roster change)
→
3. You pressed from 11-02already right → 0 written

What changed in today's record when the pattern was saved (made-up numbers, same shape):

Past days (10-25 to 10-30) are not affected by the pattern, because it starts today. They stay “off-day work”, all overtime ×1.5, even after pressing. That is on purpose: a pattern change never rewrites past days.

6Try it yourself on the test app (5174)

  1. Open http://127.0.0.1:5174/attendance/entry and scroll to the “Recompute from a date” card at the bottom.
  2. Pick a date before the old punches (e.g. 2026-10-25) and press Recompute. “Days written” should be above 0.
  3. Press again with the same date: you should see 0 days written.
  4. Pick a date in the future: a red toast says “Pick a date from the last 400 days, up to today.”
No page shows the daily records yet. The “Daily attendance” page (/attendance/days, task 5.2) will list every day with a History button per day, and this button moves there. Then you see the result directly.
